Our process

And how we work

1.0 Discovery

We will begin by gathering information and conducting primary research to gain insights into your business and your competitors, including their digital marketing strategies. We will analyze the target audience and market trends by reviewing your business goals and conducting a SWOT analysis, TWOS analysis, and PESTEL analysis. This will set the foundation for developing a comprehensive marketing strategy.

2.0 Strategic Planning

After our research and development process, we will establish the objectives and goals for the digital marketing campaign and verify the target audience. Then, we will select the most effective digital marketing channels to help your business achieve growth through our strategy.

3.0 Strategy Execution

After the discovery phase and strategic planning, we will begin the execution process, which will include content creation, campaign setup, media buying budgeting, social media marketing, email marketing and many other components. All these activities will run concurrently with SEO and SEM execution.

4.0 Measuring & Reporting

Measuring and reporting are the most important steps in the continuous improvement process. We will create monthly reports to keep your business updated on the results and will take corrective actions to optimize your campaign daily to achieve the target goals.
