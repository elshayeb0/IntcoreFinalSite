Chatbots and virtual assistants

Today, customers expect fast and personalized responses. For all businesses, to achieve high levels of customer satisfaction, it is important to act fast. With Intcore’s conversational AI and chatbots service, we help you act fast.

We build custom chatbots and virtual voice assistants that use natural language understanding (NLU) and machine learning to provide accurate, relevant, and most importantly helpful responses.

What are the key features of chatbots?

Multi language support

We build chatbots that support understanding and responding to different languages, ensuring a seamless communication process

24/7 support

Chatbots are built to always be available with no business-hour limitations, providing instant support and handling queries.

Natural language understanding (NLU)

We build chatbots that are powered by NLU to understand the user's intent and context for an enhanced experience and providing helpful solutions.

Smart analytics and reporting

You can get actionable insights on user behavior, most asked questions, and overall bot performance through analytics and reports. As well as, getting reports on the bots replies and measuring its efficiency

Why choose AI automation?

Context understanding and memory

Virtual assistants can understand context, detect user preferences, and past interactions.

Task scheduling and reminders

Virtual assistants can be asked to schedule employee meetings, cancel an event, schedule interviews, send reminders, book appointments, and manage your calendar

Seamless integration

Our team can easily integrate virtual assistants to your business CRM, calendar, HR systems, and more.

Cross-platform availability

We can integrate virtual assistants to your apps, websites, WhatsApp, slack, Microsoft teams, and any other tool that you use on daily basis.

Our Process

And how we work

  • 1.0
    Understanding Your Needs

    We start by deeply understanding your business goals, customer journey, and the problems you want the chatbot or virtual assistant to solve.

  • 2.0
    Strategy & Conversation Design

    Next, we craft a smart conversation flow, mapping how users will interact with your bot. Our team focuses on natural language, user intent, and personalization to ensure the experience feels helpful

  • 3.0
    AI Development & Integration

    We develop your chatbot or virtual assistant using cutting-edge AI technologies like Natural Language Processing (NLP) and machine learning. Whether you need a chatbot for your website, social media, or internal systems, we ensure seamless integration with your existing platforms.

  • 4.0
    Testing & Optimization

    Before launch, we test your AI solution to ensure it’s accurate, responsive, and user-friendly.

  • 5.0
    Launch & Continuous Improvement

    Once live, we monitor performance, gather feedback, and continuously optimize your chatbot or virtual assistant, keeping it aligned with evolving customer needs and business goals.

  • 6.0
    Assessment and evaluation of productivity increase

    After the implementation, our team continues to measure the effectiveness of the automation on productivity and calculate the percentage increase in efficiency or time saved
